## Rejected (with reason)
- src/store/seats.rs:4715 — The no-candidate NotFound shortcut during reconciliation lag lets a new session Y in a pane whose old seat S awaits a Move onto target T enroll a fresh seat at T, after which S's Move is refused (move_target_owned). Rejected 3-0. A session no binding names can never become a candidate, so NotFound is its final answer; before the change Y got ServiceBusy then TargetAlreadyOwned, never S's seat. Restore holds still gate Y's ordinary enrollment (TRUST-POLICY amendment; effective_recovery_disposition BaselineHeld/ExplicitHold for a pane in the baseline). The Move's evidence is keyed on X's surviving process, which is gone if Y runs in that pane. move_target_owned is pre-existing applier behaviour (seats.rs ~2752). The finding itself concedes "a plausible gap, not a reproduced failure".
- src/store/seats.rs:4291; src/service/host_evidence.rs:146 — The marker still advances only after a zero-refusal pass and the ServiceBusy gate matches any nonretired seat's binding history, so one persistently refused seat keeps resume/reattach claims for its session ServiceBusy indefinitely. Rejected 3-0. The mechanics are accurate but the change does not promise to bound every future refusal; it fixes the ordinal cause, names every refusal (StaleReason, rate-limited log, Health) and narrows the gate so unbound sessions are no longer held. Holding a session whose seat could still become the C1 candidate is the existing recovery-pending rule; answering NotFound there risks a duplicate seat, which TRUST-POLICY forbids. Advancing the marker past a refused seat would break its meaning. Out of stated scope.
- src/store/seats.rs:2534 — LATEST_BINDING_ORDER breaks ties between ended rows by wall-clock ended_at, so a backward clock step between two binding ends can rank an older ended binding above the reopened one and bring back the stale-evidence refusal. Rejected 2-1 (ground seat CONFIRM at Nit, same file:line facts; majority addresses them). The open row (ended_at NULL) always sorts first, which is the ht-kaw9 case. The failure needs a clock regression between two specific binding ends on one seat, equal-ms ties fall back to ordinal, the outcome is a named Stale refusal that the next binding end repairs, and no wrong data is written. The store orders by wall-clock timestamps elsewhere and TRUST-POLICY (~line 310) already accepts clock steps. A monotonic sequence column is a schema change beyond this fix. Possible hardening follow-up, not a gap.
- src/service/host_evidence.rs:279; src/service/host_evidence.rs:272 — Health's refused_seats list is first-seen-since-boot, capped at 8 pairs, never evicted, with no "+N more" marker, so transient early refusals can crowd out the persistently refused seat. Rejected 2-1 (ground seat CONFIRM at Nit on the missing truncation marker; majority addresses it). The cap is documented in the field doc ("later pairs are counted only in the total"), the total transitions_refused is printed beside the list, and the per-seat rate-limited lane log records every refusal regardless of the cap. Filling 8 slots needs 8 distinct transient (seat, reason) pairs before the persistent one; the scouts did not measure that this happens. Diagnostic fidelity only, no wrong state transition.
