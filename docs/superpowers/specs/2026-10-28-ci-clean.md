## Fixes

- **ht-8ln5, ht-u97r.** Budgets on real clocks are `clock.monotonic_now() + N` at the call. Proof (ht-8ln5): with 3.1 s of injected setup, the old budget fails 6 of 7 lane observer tests with the CI error; the relative one passes 7 of 7.
- **ht-jajd.** `record_failure` and `record_spawn_failure` write the lane-error log line before recording Health. Proof: with 400 ms injected before the log write, the old order fails the sweep test with the CI assertion; the new order passes.
- **ht-78f6 (busy).** `HostPort::submit_prompt_if_owed`: `NativeCli` asks `owes_wake` after its idle recheck and empty-composer read, immediately before `agent.prompt`; a declined check is a pre-send `Refused(Unavailable)`. Proof: with 3 s injected between the dispatcher's check and the submit, the old build failed the busy column 2 of 6 runs at line 1360; the fix passed 9 of 9. TRUST-POLICY.md's wake section records the recheck.
- **ht-78f6 (wake/move evidence).** The Linux release-smoke job sets `HT_HERDR_MATRIX_EVIDENCE` and `--logs-dir` and uploads `herdr-matrix-evidence-<arch>` on failure; each test's evidence now includes the daemon store and WAL (`wake_work`: `last_outcome`, `retry_step`, `reserved_at`).
- **ht-xlhl.** `docs/release.md` joins the light allowlist; the `changes` job runs `tests/release/docs_test.sh` on every push. Tag pushes stay heavy.

## Open items

- **harness-canary rulesets (user action, settings).** `scripts/canary/manifest.py check-ruleset` needs two active rulesets (Settings, Rules, Rulesets): one targeting the default branch (`~DEFAULT_BRANCH` or `refs/heads/main`) with the `deletion` and `non_fast_forward` rules, and one targeting tags `refs/tags/v*` with `deletion` and `update`. Until then the canary fails daily by design (it refuses to push the manifest). Note the tag ruleset forbids moving or deleting a `v*` tag, and the branch ruleset forbids force-pushing main.
- **ht-78f6 wake/move timeouts.** Read the next failure's evidence artifact. If `wake_work.last_outcome` is `outcome_unknown` with nothing typed, classify a prompt timeout that provably sent nothing as a pre-send refusal (fast backoff) instead of `OutcomeUnknown`.
- **ht-i4tm shard headroom.** Shard 3/5 runs ~255 s and 1/5 225-259 s against 300 s; summed test time per shard ranges 984-1707 s because `slice:` partitions by name, not time.
- **ht-btzs.** If the rustc SIGSEGV recurs, set `RUST_MIN_STACK=33554432` for release builds (rustc's own suggestion) and report upstream.
