The ground seat's dissent (TRUST-POLICY lines 65 and 102 make retire the operator's repair authority; no test covers it) is answered by the majority's reading that the resolver's own guards already govern the pane; it does not cite a requirement the design violates. At most a one-line accepted-limit note in D10 or the TRUST-POLICY text.
