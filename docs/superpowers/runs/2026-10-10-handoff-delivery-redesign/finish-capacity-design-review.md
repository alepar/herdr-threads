## CDR-1 — positive-slot continuation can still loop forever

**P1, required narrow correction.** `capacity-design.md:54–58` restricts exact-key traversal/negative-cache progress to zero slots, but `:85–90` defines positive-slot continuation as `normal_pending || current_due_exists`. These two choices are incompatible with conservative retained IDs and the same-call reclamation requirement.

Concrete state: 256 retained offers; one has canonical terminal evidence; at least one of the remaining due offers has no canonical addressed row. Reconciliation removes the terminal offer and creates a slot. There need be no new canonical message to fill it. The selected normal traversal reaches its finite end without finding the missing due ID. `current_due_exists` remains true because no exact-key attempt or negative-cache update occurred. The reply requests another quantum. On the next call, unchanged `retry_messages=true` restarts the completed normal traversal. This repeats indefinitely at a stable canonical scope. The same problem occurs with any initially nonfull ledger containing a conservative missing due ID; reclamation makes it reachable from the explicitly reviewed full-capacity state.

Source proof: `src/store/invitation_content.rs:1664` documents that missing/ambiguous canonical facts retain offers; `:1937–1946` restarts a completed empty normal walk when retry_messages is true; `src/service/delivery_presentation.rs:196` supplies retry_messages=true. No output updates the missing ID's offer stamp. The proposed exact-key miss progress is unreachable while a slot remains free. This violates the assessment/brief's finite traversal requirement and reintroduces the Watch immediate-pull resource bug.

Required correction: specify a finite positive-slot completion rule. The simplest narrow option is to stop letting due existence alone sustain positive-slot continuation after the normal finite pass has exhausted; a later admitted pull can still retry under inherited policy. If the design instead wants an exact-key follow-up, explicitly define that separate next-call mode and its finite completion state without adding a second ordinary query in the same quantum. Do not globally suppress due work at zero slots. Retain same-call reclaimed-slot resumption of the parked tuple.

Add a focused regression starting with one terminal reclamation plus conservative missing due IDs and no replacement canonical message. It must exhaust normal discovery and become quiet within a finite number of successful calls. Also cover a normal final page that consumes the last free slot so continuation uses remaining capacity, not input capacity.

## Independent findings and disposition decisions

**Process-local per-consumer negative cache: ACCEPT.** Fixed Hook/Watch slots, maximum 256 keys each, and the existing 4096-seat LRU lifetime are a minimal finite-state choice. The cache must remain a traversal hint only. The proposed incarnation plus generation and full Offer equality checks prevent an old result from suppressing a reoffered ID or a recreated same-generation ledger entry. Clearing on record/remove, pruning after retain_pending, and applying only after successful store return are necessary parts of this approval. No mutex crosses canonical store work. Native session/execution scope must actually be checked, not merely documented. Memory increases by up to two bounded trees per retained seat; allocator overhead is additional to the stated payload arithmetic.

**New 1303-probe/100-seek due budget: ACCEPT AS A DECLARED DESIGN ENVELOPE, pending executable plan/work evidence.** `src/store/effective.rs:1768–1775` has five logical relation lookups; `:1876–1883` has three physical fallback lookups; `:1997–2017` adds up to two waiver lookups. Availability at `:1811` is one indexed range seek on the logical path. Counting the initial message lookup and message/topic hydration yields 13 probes per ordinary key; three call setup probes gives 1303 for 100 attempts. Lazy needs no more than six. This is separate from reconciliation's 256 visits/1280 probes. The resolved-seat admission at `src/store/invitation_content.rs:262–270` is essential to the availability bound: do not claim the optional retirement upper-bound query has this bound for arbitrary retired seats. Schema provides unique message, prepared-recipient, receipt, lazy-recipient and current-binding keys plus seat_availability_after. SQL text and indexes support the proposal; they do not replace linked-SQLite measurements. Nonindexed work invalidates this envelope rather than authorizing a cap increase.

**Standalone-unrenderable deferral: ACCEPT under the explicitly stated scope, as a limit requiring parent disposition/documentation.** An empty-output sizing failure may defer the unchanged ID/stamp without recording an offer or settlement. A failure after earlier output must remain eligible for its standalone turn. Retry is guaranteed when the specified scope changes; it is not automatically guaranteed on every data change. In particular, the hydrator reads current topic (`src/store/queries.rs:6154`, `:5035`) and Assembler derives thread_name (`src/delivery/assemble.rs:326`), whereas the proposed scope has no topic revision. Service topic changes record invitation context without changing receipt/lazy counts (`src/store/service_controls.rs:125–135`). Thus do not claim topic-only shortening automatically invalidates a size deferral. Either accept that narrow scope limit explicitly or extend invalidation with bounded canonical context evidence and account for its cost. Any newly accepted trust limit belongs in TRUST-POLICY.md under the repository rule. No fallback text, fabricated successful delivery or larger output cap is approved.
