## Supplemental bounded coverage disposition after writer/cleanup audit

Repository-wide source/migration search found one production sweep INSERT, `schema::enqueue_warning_close_sweep:3951–4002`, and no production UPDATE/DELETE of warning_close_sweeps. Actual canonical scope ranges are therefore strictly increasing and disjoint; no runtime cleanup writer removes or rewrites them.
