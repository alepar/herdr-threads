## Required correction: cancellation after private retirement can commit

- `src/store/queries.rs:7148` retires the exact body by advancing its unread ordinal and clearing its body. When this was the final captured body and other sources are done, `done(&state)` is already true.
- The only subsequent query budget check is **inside** `while !done(&state)` at `src/store/queries.rs:7221`–7222. That check is skipped in the terminal retirement case.
- The caller installs the returned progress in memory at `src/store/invitation_content.rs:2347`–2365 and saves it at line2723. There is no post-query/pre-save cancellation check in this normal path.
- The actual store entry at `src/store/mod.rs:1296`–1306 calls `execute_decision`, not `execute_budgeted_decision`. Its writer admission checks the budget before acquiring/returning the writer (`src/store/mod.rs:354`–369), but installs no progress handler. `src/store/connection.rs:394`–403 validates, applies and commits without a further budget/cancellation check.
- Consequently, cancellation arriving during the final body's classification, or after that retirement before final persistence, can still commit the cleared cursor/skip state. Even where another source remains, cancellation after the last loop check is not fenced before final save.

This violates the supplied design's “Cancellation or any later refusal rolls all private progress back” and explicit post-retirement/pre-persistence cancellation proof obligation. It is a real normal-source path, including the all-other-sources-complete fixture used by the one-visit control. Existing admission checks and SQL-error rollback do not establish this property.

Action: add an appropriate live-budget/cancellation fence within the deciding transaction before accepting/persisting successful normal continuation progress (covering a retirement that completes the walk), and a deterministic test that cancels after classification/retirement but before final persistence. Assert the old private cursor, partial/skip state and captured tuple remain exact and retry succeeds. No broad cancellation redesign is required by this review.

The new `tests/store/invitation_content.rs:1376` control injects a trigger that raises ABORT during the final UPDATE. That correctly targets storage refusal rollback, but cancellation never raises that trigger and is not tested there.
