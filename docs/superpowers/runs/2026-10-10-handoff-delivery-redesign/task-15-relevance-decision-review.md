2. **The wake counter is not yet a proven presentation epoch producer.** The packet correctly refuses to assume every relevance-changing mutation increments `wake_work.attention_version`. Parent/planner are considering a dedicated observed relevance episode; its exact Open/Absent/Unknown transitions, cross-consumer identity, binding reset, monotonic checked overflow and coalescing limits must be specified. This is a pending selection, not a claim of current source corruption.
