## Explicit coordinator-approved compatibility limits

The client cannot establish that the canonical recipient audience is entirely human-waived. Therefore an ordinary send that may create an agent obligation requires the capability even if its eventual audience is all human. A human sender is never treated as a recipient waiver. Server-side human waivers remain unchanged and lazy sends are exempt.

Ordinary pending local intents do not cache a committed result, and actual public `Command::OperationStatus` dispatch refuses with `Unauthorized: verified operation scope required`; the public wire cannot supply that scope. Service pending replays likewise conservatively refuse without locally retained completion; this is not a claim that the service protocol lacks status inspection. Thus a commit-unknown ordinary CLI or service retry must conservatively refuse on an unsupported daemon, even if the original might have committed. Completed retained compound outcomes and completed service journal records remain inspectable unchanged without new send effects. A proposed fixture-only public status success was rejected before implementation because the actual dispatcher cannot do it. TRUST-POLICY A6 records both accepted limits; no new inspection protocol or heuristic was added.
