## Spec and authority

The patch closes the exact ordering gap. `pull` reads the frontier and makes its one relevance advance before allocating and settling notices (`invitation_content.rs:1965–1980`, `:2396–2404`). A Notice/Attention page can therefore obsolete its own Open notice witness after the proof has already run. The new predicate advertises further work only if the page actually carried notices and still has an active episode to reevaluate. It does not assume settlement implies absence, manufacture a Clear, or substitute output history for canonical authority.

The existing `state.outbox_more = i64::from(nonordinary_more)` persists this trigger with the allocated outbox (`:2470`). Fresh `page.more` carries the same value. `replay` uses `state.outbox_more` alongside current bounded proof/publication/ordinary continuation (`:1659–1662`); a lost response therefore retains the continuation obligation with the original token instead of needing an unrelated notification. Watch confirms a successfully written/flushed page and then follows `page.more` (`src/cli/watch.rs:667–707`). Successful confirmation retires the outbox and resets its stored continuation flag (`invitation_content.rs:767–796`), while the already-received page still schedules its next Pull.

All admitting and settlement authority remains in the existing deciding transaction. The patch evaluates two in-memory values already produced there. The exact generation/execution frontier writer, bounded oldest-first carried-prefix validation, fixed Hook/Watch consumer rows and token admission are unchanged. TRUST-POLICY's one bounded advance per successful Pull/replay and zero-discovery explicit Confirm rules are preserved; no new provenance or accepted-limit amendment is needed.
