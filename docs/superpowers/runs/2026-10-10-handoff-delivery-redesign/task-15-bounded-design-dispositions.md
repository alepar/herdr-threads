## Preferred cohesive public V2 wire

Actual `src/protocol/delivery.rs::DeliveryPull` is deny_unknown_fields and contains claim, optional seen_attention_version and marker_prefix (<=8 args/4096 bytes). DeliveryPage has attention_version/text/offered/truncated/more, no page token. DeliveryConfirm confirms MessageIds and must retain receipt semantics.

Prefer a new capability-gated V2 command/result rather than silently adding fields to V1 requests. Proposed `DeliveryPullV2` has canonical claim, fixed consumer enum Hook/Watch, optional confirmed presentation token, and bounded marker_prefix; reply Page carries a unique binding/consumer-scoped presentation token plus bounded content/continuation. An explicit idempotent `DeliveryPresentationConfirmV2` provides post-output confirmation; confirmed token on a later pull may be an equivalent retry path. Exact names/serde shapes remain fresh-review decisions. OldV1 fields/bytes/admission/semantics remain unchanged; capable consumers select V2 only after advertised support. No overloaded attention_version or DeliveryConfirm message IDs.

Store keeps at most one durable outstanding page per binding/fixed consumer. Allocation atomically stores payload/token and advances item/cursor state; retransmission returns that allocation until validated confirmation. No transaction spans stdout/socket I/O. Confirmation validates canonical instance/seat/generation/execution/consumer and exact outstanding token, is idempotent for its retained last-confirmed token, and cannot confirm an arbitrary future/other-consumer page. Wrong tokens do not advance state. Stale binding confirmation cannot affect its successor. Proposed token is opaque, not inferred from content_version or decision_seq.

Hook admission preserves `service/delivery_pull.rs::hook_pull_view`: top-level matching instance, resolved/nonretired/nonheld seat, cooperative canonical binding, HookPull declaration and exact native session. Watch admission independently reuses canonical existing watch admission; it MUST NOT weaken HookPull restrictions to admit Watch. Fixed consumer kinds prevent an unbounded client-created consumer namespace. At most one active watch owner per binding; replacement/connection ownership must reuse existing mod-channel ownership rules and invalidate stale watch owners. Hook and Watch presentation progress/outbox are independent, so one cannot consume the other's context. Wire/body limits and explicit target/default-feature accounting require review before adding command variants.

V2 Hook returns existing hook message/notice behavior plus the bounded invitation content path. V2 Watch uses the invitation content path while ordinary message watch output retains existing wire semantics. Avoid reading invitations through both old InboxBatchV2 drain and V2: capable watch drain must suppress the old invitation output branch; capable hook must likewise avoid duplicate invitation allocation. Preserve V1 fallback behavior honestly, without claiming its saturation/restart guarantees are upgraded.
