decision: redesign
summary: C1 reattachment was implemented as a seatless decide_continuity that ends the old binding and opens none, plus a separate ordinary check-in and an every-event instance-wide intent scan for lost replies; that split causes most hook/journal findings and evidence (b). Fold the binding open into the deciding transaction and delete the per-event replay scan; the other clusters are patch sweeps.
pattern: Every-event journal scan (r1 Nit hook.rs:924/journal.rs:580), its FIFO/symlink exposure (r1 FYI journal.rs:769), whole-scan failure (r1 Nit journal.rs:766/:787), four findings rejected on "the next hook event opens the binding" (hook.rs:942, :1092, :924/:925, :1132), refuted by evidence (b): hook.rs ~1356 refuses "local context differs from current service mapping" before stale-context retirement, intent already completed, seat resolved with no open binding. Spec §3 said the reply returns seat + binding generation written "exactly as an ordinary lifecycle check-in does"; implementation (seats.rs:3748-3750) ends the binding and leaves the successor to a second request. Separate rule-shaped clusters: diagnostic read on the fenced path; incomplete carry-forward (C4); indirect open-binding lookup; inconsistent client A4 heuristic. Evidence (a) and (c) share one cause: decisions before the first reconciliation pass of the epoch are treated as final.

Spec §3: "the deciding transaction opens the successor binding"; replace the "Lost reply: before find_seat, every hook event … replays" sentence with "a lost reply is recovered by the committed binding; the intent is retried only within the same hook".
